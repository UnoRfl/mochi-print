Printer not showing up?
  1. It won't appear in your phone's Bluetooth settings. That's normal, it only shows up when you tap Connect printer here.
  2. Close the Fun Print app completely (swipe it away). The printer can only talk to one app at a time and hides while Fun Print is connected.
  3. Turn the printer on (or wake the camera's print mode) and keep it near your phone.
  4. On Android, turn on Bluetooth and Location. Chrome needs Location on to find Bluetooth gadgets.
  5. Unplug the camera from any computer's USB cable.
